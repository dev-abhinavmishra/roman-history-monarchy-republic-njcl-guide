Roman Monarchy & Republic — Kings, Consuls & Family Tree Explorer. An offline, single-file study aid for the NJCL National Roman Civilization Exam, covering Rome from Romulus (753 BC) through the seven kings and the Republic to Octavian becoming Augustus (27 BC) — where the companion Roman Emperors tool begins. Compiled from Wikipedia, Livy, Plutarch, Polybius, Encyclopædia Britannica, and Cary & Scullard's A History of Rome. For Republican statesmen, the timeline “reign” bar marks each figure's period of greatest influence. See the ⓘ About panel for full data provenance.
